🔑 Key Language

Key Language: Likes and Dislikes

Use verbs such as "like," "enjoy," and "hate" to give your opinion on workplace activities. These can be followed by a noun, or by a gerund ("-ing" form of the verb) and a noun.

I don't want to work in IT. I hate computers.
verb + noun
I want to be a sales manager. I like giving presentations.
verb + gerund + noun
10.2 Further examples: I enjoy business trips.  ·  She hates writing contracts.  ·  He doesn't like interviews.  ·  I like meeting clients.

10.3 How to Form Likes and Dislikes

SubjectVerb(Gerund)Noun
Ilike / enjoy / don't like / hate—business trips.
Ilike / enjoy / don't like / hategivingpresentations.

Add "-ing" to the verb to form the gerund when talking about an activity.
